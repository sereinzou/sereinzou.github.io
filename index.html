01 / INTRODUCTION

A little about me.

My work sits at the intersection of numerical mathematics, scientific computing, and computational electromagnetics. I am interested in designing accurate and robust methods for problems with complex geometries and material interfaces.

In particular, I study finite element methods for Maxwell eigenvalue problems and the computation of photonic crystal band structures.
